# Guitar samples

Steel-string acoustic guitar (General MIDI program 26), one note every major third from E2 (MIDI 40) to
G♯5/A♭5 (MIDI 80), named by MIDI note number. The app plays a chord's notes from the nearest sample,
resampled by at most two semitones (`src/lib/music/guitar.ts`).

Source: the *Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont* by Frank Wen, released by its author under the MIT license,
as pre-rendered by Benjamin Gleitzman's [MIDI.js Soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts)
(`FluidR3_GM/acoustic_guitar_steel-mp3`; that rendering is published under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0).
Converted here to mono 64 kb/s MP3, cut to 2.6 s with a 0.4 s fade-out.
